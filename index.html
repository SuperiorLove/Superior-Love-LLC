About Superior Love

SUPERIOR LOVE has been providing personalized in-home care and residential living since 1992. We became certified 2003, bringing it into fruition 2012. Our care team is dedicated to providing Superior Love and care, assuring residents feel safe, comfortable, and well cared for. Superior Love has on-call medical partners ready to deliver attentive care 24-hours daily support while encouraging independence.

Superior Love was inspired by assisting my mom in caring for her parents(my grandparents). We provided them the best care possible, while making sure they still lived life on their own terms, still being able to enjoy social outings and activities. My assignment was being a great companion. Listening to them tell memories of their upbringing, careers, and dating life. It meant so much to my grandparents, that I was genuinely invested in spending quality time with them and looked forward to the next story they had to tell. My grandparents made me feel special. With the look of joy and gratitude their faces expressed every time I kept them company. I would get spoiled with pecan sandies, werthers candies, Eskimo bars and popsicles. I realized the importance of genuine quality care as I assisted my mom with preparing meals, folding laundry, organizing medication, picking out their wardrobes for family outings and being a big help to my mom. The impact of serving to me has always felt rewarding and fulfilling. My mom taught me not only how to love and care. But honoring people with respect, value, and responsibility. That is why I love superiorly with superior love.

What We Offer

Our Care Options

Thoughtful care that meets residents and families exactly where they are.

🏠

Independent Living

A maintenance free lifestyle offering private and semi private suites to active seniors (55+) living an autonomous , medical assistance free lifestyle.

  • Social programming & life enrichment activities
  • Housekeeping and laundry services
  • Meal plans with dietary accommodations
  • Transportation coordination
  • Who it's for: Active seniors needing minimal daily assistance
🩹

Assisted Living

We offer assisted living designed as residential home options for seniors who need a little extra hand with activities of daily living.

  • 24-hour care and supervision
  • Assistance with activities of daily living
  • Medication management and reminders
  • Individualized care plans in coordination with healthcare providers
  • Who it's for: Seniors needing regular daily assistance
Specialized
🧠

Memory Care

Designed for individuals living with alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia. We offer daily structured routines and specialized support. Provided in a residential environment designed to secure safety of residents 24/7 while promoting familiarity and comfort. Through personalized care assistance, and passionate care aids, while remaining connected to a social and community life.

  • Secure environment with specialized safety measures
  • Cognitive engagement programming
  • 24-hour supervision and behavioral support
  • Regular family communication and support
  • Who it's for: Individuals with Alzheimer's, dementia, or related conditions
🥪

Respite Care

Short- term care relief for primary caregivers. Encouraging caregivers to a stress free time away from loved ones. To catch up on rest, handle personal matters, or traveling. We can assure loved ones will receive professional supervision and assistance.

  • Flexible duration — hours to several weeks
  • All personal care and daily living assistance
  • Companionship and social engagement
  • Planned or emergency availability
  • Who it's for: Family caregivers who need temporary relief
❄

Hospice Care

Supportive Medical care for residents enduring life-limiting illness. Our job is to comfort and ease residents with terminal illness. Medical trained staff will provide symptom management to relieve pain. Focusing on the residents comfort and quality of life rather than cure.

  • Pain and symptom management
  • Emotional and spiritual counseling
  • Family support services and bereavement follow-up
  • Available in-home, in-facility, or preferred setting
  • Who it's for: Individuals with a terminal diagnosis and their families
